OD&D combat tester: grid

Man-to-man on a dungeon map: 10' squares of 3⅓' cells, every inch of movement counted, targets set by hand, facing from position.

A red F or R on a figure means it is attacking its target's flank or rear; the selected figure's panel lists everyone it is engaged with. Tap or click a figure to select it; tap a highlighted cell to move it (figures may pass through friends, not enemies). To target: with "Tap enemy to target" on, select your figure and tap an enemy; on a keyboard, hover an enemy and press T, or right-click it.
