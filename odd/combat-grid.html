OD&D combat tester: grid

Man-to-man on a dungeon map: 10' squares of 3⅓' cells, every inch of movement counted, targets set by hand, facing from position.

Click a figure to select it; click a highlighted cell to move it (figures may pass through friends, not enemies). Hover an enemy and press T to target it, or right-click it. Esc clears the selection.

Log

Place both sides, then start the fight.
